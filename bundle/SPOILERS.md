# Author's guide — major spoilers

Do not read this before a first playthrough unless investigating a bug.

## The central reveal

Meridian's census is not merely identifying people. Its reference service uses coerced memories to improve its returns and rewrites the index to support administrative certainty. The player is connected to an early return record. That does not remove agency, prove every resident hostile, or excuse the officials who choose to disappear people. The independent film, physical carbon, private recollections and witnessed wiring sheet form a chain the compromised terminal cannot amend.

## Seven nights

1. **First impressions:** Ada teaches the personal-memory loop. A false Tomas has a detectable appearance error; Mara's expired permit and Ivo's lie are human problems.
2. **Relationships:** Nessa and Lio, the gardener's network and conflicting household claims make witnesses useful but fallible.
3. **The record:** Microfilm contradicts the census. A purported return repeats a human's earlier grief-related mistake, revealing imitation rather than simple dishonesty.
4. **Inside/outside:** Power fails; the telephone and window disagree; the real injured Mara follows an impersonator. Denying her now has an irreversible consequence.
5. **Authority:** Orders conflict with evidence. Selene's index changes during the interview while her carbon does not. Dalia's complicity becomes harder to avoid.
6. **Consent and memory:** Orin openly describes his origin; two Micahs disagree on a witnessed mechanical detail. Anja's paper explains the physical feeds.
7. **The last signature:** Collaborators return, prior memories become decisive, and the last queue gives way to a choice about the system itself.

## Ending routes

- **The Locked Dawn / Survivor:** Keep infiltration below the breach threshold and choose accountable perimeter protection. Three scene actions establish the shelter watch and leave a handwritten ledger.
- **The Quiet Building / Purge:** With a tenable perimeter, countersign the administrative sweep. This is a deliberate harmful course, not a “high score” reward.
- **Every Window Lit / Breach:** Five admitted hostile Echoes force evacuation. Disconnecting ventilation on the true route can also destroy the perimeter and lead here.
- **The Other Frequency / Whistleblower:** Keep the film, Selene's carbon and broadcast evidence; admit/protect Emil so his transmitter is ready and he is inside. Transmit the evidence rather than editing out the scientist's responsibility.
- **A Chair at the Table / Coexistence:** Retain consent and independent garden evidence, admit Orin on the final night, and retain community confidence of at least 25. Voluntary supervision addresses conduct, not compulsory deception about origin.
- **The Unrecorded Morning / True ending:** Retain film, carbon, the player's historical record, final disclosure, circuit sheet and consent. Protect/admit Tomas, Anja and Dalia and secure the relay/key flags. Follow the independent physical diagram: A ventilation, B alarm, C census reference. B is reversible; A causes a dangerous emergency; C disconnects the coercive reference service without killing the people it copied.

## Content continuity examples

Ada's penny boats and desk leg; Pavel's seven-star cup versus a six-star account; Ivo's private name for a lift; Celia's chair/seat phrase; Micah's two alarm bells versus three. These are authored memories or observations, not random trivia that exists only in the answer key. Read the earlier actual dialogue nodes when editing a later return. The memory award must follow presentation of the detail.

The scanner is intentionally not definitive. For a solvability review, compare a person's claimed identity, actual document/live discrepancy, independent expectation and mundane explanation; do not substitute a high reading or an aggressive mood for reasoning.
