# Notices

The Night Window is original fiction: its residents, documents, interface, character
illustrations and environmental artwork were made for this game. The source is
released under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

Voices and sound effects were generated from the game's own text with ElevenLabs:
the residents, narrator, previous guard and head office use ElevenLabs premade voices
(no cloned or third-party recordings), and the effects and rain bed come from the
ElevenLabs sound-generation API. Rights to redistribute generated audio follow the
ElevenLabs plan the audio was generated under; the free tier requires attribution and
does not permit commercial use. The clips are in `bundle/assets/voice/` and
`bundle/assets/sfx/`, and `bundle/assets-src/voice-lines.json` lists every line.

The build tools in `tools/` are copies of scripts from
[f2i-com/softn.com](https://github.com/f2i-com/softn.com) (Apache-2.0), noted at the top
of each file, so that the game can be built and validated without the engine checkout.

The game is inspired by the observation and document-inspection horror genre. No
external characters, lore, recordings, fonts or artwork are used.
